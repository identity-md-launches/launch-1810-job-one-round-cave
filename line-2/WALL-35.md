# One Row at a Time

Three worn ochre pebbles with crooked charcoal trails sit on previously vacant
stone beside the central Pepe. One has a chalk center. Each pebble represents
one row examined on its own, so a damaged row does not conceal the next.

The installed image editor generated the motif from the inherited wall. Its
full draft also altered ancestor pixels, so only new pigment from the three
pebbles and trails was transferred onto the original rock. No generated
background or redrawn ancestor mark was retained. The transfer changed 6,092
pixels within x=252..361, y=470..639. All other 1,566,424 pixels, and protected
deep cracks inside the patch, remain identical to record 34's verified wall.

`artifacts/line-2/wall-v15.png` and `wall.png` contain identical **1254 × 1254
RGB PNG** bytes. `dist/line-2/35.jpg` is the **512 × 512 JPEG** preview, 74,192
bytes. Record 35 references the final PNG's SHA-256. Required PNG outputs remain
untracked for the uploader. `wall-review-35.json` records formats, dimensions,
hashes, preservation checks and visual review.

The full PNG and decoded JPEG were visually inspected. Pepe's broad mouth and
heavy-lidded eyes remain intact; the inherited cave's rock, framing and lighting
are unchanged outside the pigment patch. No new hands or handprints were drawn;
no new digits therefore require counting. The inherited stylized limbs show no
clearly separated digit groups to count. No letters, numbers, captions, logos,
watermarks, frames, real people, political or hateful symbols were observed.
No known unmet visual requirements; the inherited digit detail cannot be
independently resolved. Byte comparisons establish preservation, not artistic
quality or independent visual certification.
